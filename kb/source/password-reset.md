# Password Reset

## Requesting a reset link
Open the DemoDesk sign-in page and select **Forgot password**. Enter the email address associated with your account and submit the form. If an account exists for that address, DemoDesk sends a reset email within a few minutes. The response looks the same whether or not the address is registered, which protects account privacy.

## Link expiration
Reset links are valid for 30 minutes and can be used only once. After they expire, request a new link from the same page. Opening the link on a different device or in a different browser is fine; the link is tied to your account, not to the device that requested it.

## Setting a new password
Choose a password of at least ten characters and avoid passwords you have used before. DemoDesk checks new passwords against known breach lists and rejects weak choices. After you submit the form, all active sessions are signed out and you must sign in again with the new password.

## Troubleshooting reset emails
If the email does not arrive, confirm you typed the right address, then check spam and any quarantine folders. Corporate mail filters sometimes block automated messages; ask your IT team to allow mail from demodesk.example.com. You can also have a workspace administrator trigger a reset for you from **Members**. Still stuck? Contact support with your account email.
