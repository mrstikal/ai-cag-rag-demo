# Tax and VAT

## VAT for EU customers
Prices on our website exclude VAT. Customers in the EU are charged VAT at their local rate unless a valid VAT ID is on file, in which case the reverse charge applies and no VAT is added. VAT is shown as a separate line on each invoice.

## Adding a VAT ID
Add your VAT ID under Settings > Billing > Tax ID. We validate it against the VIES database within one business day. Once validated, the ID applies to future invoices and the tax line is removed from renewals.

## Tax-exempt organizations
Charities, schools, and government entities can apply for tax exemption by uploading a valid exemption certificate under Settings > Billing > Tax ID. Approved exemptions apply from the next billing cycle, and tax already charged can be refunded after review.
