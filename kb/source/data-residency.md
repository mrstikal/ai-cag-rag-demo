# Data Residency

## Available regions

DemoDesk offers four data regions: United States (Virginia), European Union (Frankfurt), United Kingdom (London), and Australia (Sydney). Workspace content, files, and backups are stored at rest in the selected region. Metadata used for authentication may be replicated globally to keep sign-in fast.

## Choosing a region

You select a region when creating a workspace. Choose the region that matches your legal, contractual, or latency requirements. For customers in the EEA, the Frankfurt region is usually the right choice. The region cannot be changed from the settings; moving requires a migration request.

## Moving your workspace between regions

Region migrations are available on Business and Enterprise plans. Open a support request with your target region, and we will schedule the move during a maintenance window. Migrations typically complete within 48 hours, and the workspace is read-only during the final cutover. After the move, old-region backups expire on their normal schedule.
