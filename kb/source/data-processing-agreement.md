# Data Processing Agreement

## When a DPA is needed

A Data Processing Agreement (DPA) is required whenever your organization processes personal data through DemoDesk and is acting as a data controller. This applies to most business customers, including teams storing customer contact details or employee information in the workspace.

## Requesting a DPA

Owners and admins can generate our standard DPA from Settings > Legal > Data Processing Agreement. The document is filled with your organization details automatically and can be downloaded for signature. For negotiated terms, contact your account manager and allow a few weeks for legal review.

## Standard contractual clauses

Our DPA incorporates the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses for transfers of personal data outside the EEA. The relevant modules are selected based on your configuration. SCCs are included by reference, so you do not need a separate agreement to cover international transfers. Our privacy team can confirm the applicable module for your setup.
