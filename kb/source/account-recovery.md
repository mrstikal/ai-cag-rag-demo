# Account Recovery

## When to use account recovery
Account recovery restores access when you can no longer sign in and a normal password reset is not enough — for example, when you have forgotten which email you used, changed employers, or no longer control the inbox tied to the account. Use recovery instead of repeatedly guessing passwords, which can trigger a temporary lock.

## Recovery via verified email
If you still control a verified email on the account, select **Forgot password** and request a reset link. This is the fastest path and usually restores access in minutes. Once inside, update your email address and password right away so the recovery link cannot be reused.

## Recovery when you lost access to your email
If the inbox is unavailable — an old company address, for instance — signing in is impossible, but recovery is not. Start the recovery form and provide the account email, your full name, and the workspace name. DemoDesk matches these details against the account record and sets up an identity review.

## Identity verification with support
During review, support may ask for the last four digits of the card on file, the month and amount of your most recent invoice, and the date you created the workspace. Reviews typically complete within two business days. For security, recovery never bypasses two-factor authentication without the same verification.
