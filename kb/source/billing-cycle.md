# Billing Cycle

## When you are charged
Monthly subscriptions are charged on the same date each month as the original signup, and annual subscriptions are charged on the signup anniversary each year. If your billing date falls on the 29th, 30th, or 31st, we charge on the last day of shorter months.

## Changing your billing date
Your billing date cannot be changed directly. If the date is inconvenient, contact support and we can align renewals to the 1st of the month by applying a prorated credit. The change takes effect on your next renewal.

## Grace period for failed renewals
If a renewal charge fails, we retry the payment three times over seven days and email the billing contact each time. Your workspace stays active during this grace period. If all retries fail, the workspace is suspended until payment succeeds, and data is retained for 30 days.
