# Creating an Account

## Signup steps
Open the DemoDesk signup page and enter your work email address, full name, and a password of at least ten characters. Accept the terms of service and select **Create account**. You can also sign up with a Google or Microsoft work account; in that case DemoDesk uses your existing profile details and no separate password is created.

## Email verification
After signup, DemoDesk sends a verification message to the address you entered. Open it and select **Verify email** within 24 hours. Until verification is complete, your workspace is created but you cannot invite teammates or connect integrations. If the message does not arrive, check spam and request a new link from the signup screen.

## Choosing a plan
New accounts start on a 14-day trial of the Team plan, which includes unlimited projects and five seats. You can switch to Free, Business, or Enterprise at any time from **Settings > Billing**. Downgrades take effect at the end of the current billing period, and your data is retained on every plan.

## Deleting an accidental signup
If you created an account by mistake, go to **Settings > Account** and choose **Delete account**. Deletion removes your profile, workspace, and associated data after a 7-day grace period, during which signing in cancels the request. If you signed up with the wrong email but want to keep the workspace, change the account email instead of deleting.
