# Changing Your Email Address

## Changing the account email
Open **Settings > Account** and select **Change email**. Enter the new address and confirm with your current password. If your workspace enforces single sign-on, the email field is managed by your identity provider and must be changed there instead.

## Confirmation from both addresses
After you submit the change, DemoDesk sends a confirmation message to both the old and the new address. Both links must be opened within 24 hours; this prevents someone who briefly gained access to your session from silently redirecting your account. Until both confirmations complete, the old address remains active for sign-in.

## What happens to notifications
Once confirmed, all notification email — product updates, billing receipts, and security alerts — is delivered to the new address. Email forwarding rules you created in DemoDesk are disabled automatically, and reports previously sent to the old address must be re-subscribed. Workspace administrators see a notice in the audit log when a member changes their email.
