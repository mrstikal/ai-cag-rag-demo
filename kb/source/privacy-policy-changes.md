# Privacy Policy Changes

## How we notify you

When we make material changes to the privacy policy, we notify workspace owners and admins by email and show an in-app banner the next time they sign in. Non-material changes, such as clarifications or formatting fixes, are published without a dedicated notification. You can subscribe to policy updates from the notification settings.

## Effective dates

Every version of the policy shows an effective date at the top. Material changes take effect no earlier than 30 days after we notify you, unless a shorter period is required by law. Continuing to use DemoDesk after the effective date means you accept the updated policy. If you disagree, you can export your data and close your account before the change takes effect.

## Historical versions

Previous versions of the privacy policy are archived and available on request from the privacy team. Each archived copy keeps its original effective date so you can compare versions. We keep at least five years of history. Customers under a DPA may receive change notices through their account manager as well.
