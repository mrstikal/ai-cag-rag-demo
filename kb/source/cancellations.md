# Cancelling a Subscription

## How to cancel
Cancel from Settings > Billing > Cancel plan. A workspace admin must confirm the cancellation. Your plan stays active until the end of the current billing period, and you are not charged again after that. Cancelling does not trigger an automatic refund. Admins can schedule a cancellation in advance, and the workspace shows the exact end date before you confirm.

## What happens to your data
You keep full access to your data until the period ends. After cancellation, data is retained for 30 days in case you reactivate, then permanently deleted. Export tickets and reports before the retention window closes.

## Reactivating
To reactivate, sign in and choose a plan before the 30-day retention window ends. Your workspace, settings, and data are restored as they were. After deletion, reactivation starts a new, empty workspace.
