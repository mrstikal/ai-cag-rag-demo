# Notification Settings

## Email notifications

Email notifications are configured per user from Settings > Notifications. You can choose which events send email, including mentions, assignments, comments on your items, and workspace announcements. Security alerts, such as new sign-ins from unknown devices, are always sent and cannot be turned off.

## In-app notifications

In-app notifications appear in the bell menu and update in real time. Clicking a notification opens the related item directly. Preferences for in-app alerts are set separately from email, so you can receive alerts in the app without email noise. Unread counts are per workspace.

## Digest frequency

Instead of individual emails, you can receive a digest that bundles activity. Options are immediate, daily, or weekly. Digests are sent at 8:00 in your local time zone. Mentions and direct assignments are delivered immediately even when a digest is enabled, so urgent items are never delayed.

## Muting notifications

Mute a project, task, or thread from its menu to stop alerts while staying a member. You can mute all non-critical notifications during focus hours. Admins can set workspace-wide defaults and restrict which events can send email to keep inboxes manageable.
