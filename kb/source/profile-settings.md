# Profile Settings

## Display name and avatar
Update your display name under **Settings > Profile**. The name appears on comments, mentions, and activity reports across the workspace. Upload an avatar as a PNG or JPEG up to 2 MB; if you skip it, DemoDesk shows your initials. Avatars and display names are visible to everyone in your workspace.

## Time zone and language
Set your time zone so deadlines, meeting notes, and scheduled reports line up with your local clock. DemoDesk detects a default from your browser, but you can override it at any time. Interface language is set separately and applies only to your own account, not to teammates or shared content.

## Account preferences
Profile preferences include notification frequency (instant, daily digest, or off), the start day of your week, and whether DemoDesk remembers your last-used workspace on sign-in. Marketing emails are controlled by a separate toggle and never affect security or billing notices, which are always delivered regardless of other preferences.
