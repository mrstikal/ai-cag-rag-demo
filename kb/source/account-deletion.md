# Account Deletion

## Requesting deletion

You can request deletion from Settings > Account > Delete Account, or by contacting support. We ask you to confirm the request by email to prevent mistakes. If you are the workspace owner, transfer ownership first or the workspace will be scheduled for deletion along with your account.

## Grace period

Deletion starts with a 30-day grace period. Your account is deactivated immediately, but you can cancel the request during those 30 days by signing in and choosing Restore Account. After the grace period ends, deletion becomes permanent and cannot be undone.

## What is deleted

After the grace period we delete your profile, email address, personal settings, active sessions, and API keys. If the workspace is deleted, all its content is removed as well, including projects, tasks, comments, and attachments. Deletion is recorded in the audit log for accountability.

## What is retained

Some records must be kept for legal and financial reasons. Billing records and invoices are retained for the period required by tax law, typically seven years. Anonymized usage statistics may be kept. Encrypted backups that still contain your data are purged when the backup retention window closes, within 90 days.
