# Billing Overview

## How billing works
DemoDesk bills per workspace based on the number of active agent seats and your plan. Charges are calculated at the start of each billing period for the seats active at that time. Changing seat count mid-cycle creates a prorated line item on your next invoice, up or down.

## Monthly vs annual
Monthly plans renew every month on the date you subscribed and cost the listed monthly price. Annual plans are billed once per year at a 20 percent discount, which works out to roughly two months free. You can switch from monthly to annual at any time from Settings > Billing.

## Updating payment details
Update your card or billing address under Settings > Billing > Payment methods. Changes apply to future charges; a failed charge from the current cycle still needs to be resolved. Only workspace admins can edit payment details.
