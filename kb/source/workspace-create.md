# Creating a Workspace

## Creating your first workspace

Sign in and choose Create Workspace from the workspace switcher. You can start blank or from a template such as Support or Project Management. The creator becomes the workspace owner and can invite others immediately. You can belong to multiple workspaces and switch between them from the top bar.

## Naming and region

Pick a display name and a URL slug; the slug must be unique across DemoDesk. You also choose the data region for the workspace, which determines where content is stored. The region cannot be changed later without a migration. See Data Residency for guidance on choosing a region.

## Inviting the first members

Invite people by email from Settings > Members. Choose a role for each invite: admin or member. Invitees receive an email and join after accepting. On paid plans you can also enable SSO so members are created automatically at first sign-in. Member limits depend on your plan.
