# Managing Active Sessions

## Viewing active sessions
Open **Settings > Security > Active sessions** to see every device currently signed in to your account. Each entry shows the device and browser, an approximate location, and the time of the most recent activity. Sessions older than 30 days are cleaned up automatically.

## Revoking a session
Select **Revoke** next to any session you do not recognize. The device is signed out immediately, and the next request from it redirects to the sign-in page. Revoking a session does not change your password or affect other devices. If you see sessions you cannot explain, revoke them all and reset your password.

## Signing out everywhere
**Sign out everywhere** ends every session except the one you are using right now. Use it after working on a shared computer, after offboarding a team member, or whenever you suspect your credentials were exposed. Administrators can also sign a member out of all devices from **Members**, which is useful when someone leaves the company.
