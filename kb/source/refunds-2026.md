# Refund Policy

## Eligibility
Customers may request a refund within 14 days after the initial purchase. Refunds apply only to the first purchase of a subscription; later charges are not covered. Requests are evaluated against the date of the initial purchase, not the date of first contact.

## Renewals
Subscription renewals are normally non-refundable. If you cancel before the renewal date, no further charge is made, but charges already processed for a renewal are not refunded. Annual plans follow the same rule: the initial term qualifies, later renewals do not.

## How to request a refund
Contact support with the invoice number and the reason for the request. We review every request within two business days and reply to the email address on the account.

## Processing time
Approved refunds are returned to the original payment method within 5 to 10 business days. Depending on your bank, the credit may take one extra statement cycle to appear.
