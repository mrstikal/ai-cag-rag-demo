# Invoices

## Where to find invoices
Invoices are available under Settings > Billing > Invoices for every charge on your account. Workspace admins and billing contacts can view them. Each invoice shows its status: paid, open, or failed, so you can spot problems at a glance.

## Invoice fields
Every invoice lists the invoice number, issue date, billing period, plan, agent seat count, subtotal, taxes, and total. Annual invoices also show the discount applied compared with monthly billing.

## Downloading a PDF
Open an invoice and click Download PDF to save a copy for your records. PDFs are generated at the time of payment and can be downloaded again at any time. If a PDF is missing for an older charge, contact support with the invoice number.

## Updating billing details on an invoice
Company name, address, and VAT ID appear as they were entered when the invoice was issued. To correct details on a past invoice, update them under Settings > Billing > Billing details, then contact support to reissue the document.
