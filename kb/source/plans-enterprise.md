# Enterprise Plan

## What's included
Enterprise pricing is custom and includes unlimited agents and ticket volume, advanced security controls, audit logs, custom data retention, and a dedicated success manager. Enterprise also adds a 99.9 percent uptime SLA, priority phone support, and quarterly business reviews.

## SSO and compliance
Enterprise supports SAML single sign-on with Okta, Azure AD, and OneLogin, plus SCIM user provisioning. We maintain SOC 2 Type II and can sign a HIPAA BAA or DPA on request. Security questionnaires are handled by our compliance team during evaluation.

## Contacting sales
Contact sales@demodesk.example or book a demo from the Pricing page. The sales team responds within one business day and can provide a sandbox workspace, a formal quote, and a trial of up to 30 days before you commit to an annual contract.
