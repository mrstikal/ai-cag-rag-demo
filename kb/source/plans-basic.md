# Basic Plan

## What's included
The Basic plan costs $19 per agent per month and includes email and web form ticketing, a branded help center, canned responses, and standard reporting. Every new workspace starts with a 14-day trial of Pro features and moves to Basic automatically when the trial ends, unless you upgrade first.

## Usage limits
Basic supports up to 5 agents and 1,000 new tickets per month. Attachments are capped at 25 MB per file with 5 GB of total storage, and tickets older than 12 months are archived and become read-only. Additional agent seats are not available on Basic; upgrade to Pro to add them.

## Who it's for
Basic is built for small support teams that handle fewer than 1,000 tickets a month and do not need automation, SLA policies, or API access. If your team grows past five agents or you need custom domains and integrations, upgrade to Pro from Settings > Billing at any time.
