# Refund Policy (2025)

This policy applied to purchases made in 2025. It was superseded by the current Refund Policy on 2026-01-01.

## Eligibility
Customers may request a refund within 30 days after the initial purchase. Refunds apply only to the first purchase of a subscription; charges made after the 30-day window are not refundable under this policy.

## Renewals
Subscription renewals are normally non-refundable, even if the subscription is cancelled shortly after the renewal date. If a renewal was charged in error, contact support within 7 days and we will review the case.

## How to request a refund
Email support@demodesk.example with the invoice number and the reason for your request. We review requests within two business days and reply to the email address on the account.

## Processing time
Approved refunds are returned to the original payment method within 5 to 10 business days. Depending on your bank, the refund may take one extra statement cycle to appear.
