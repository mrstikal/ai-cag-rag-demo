# Upgrades and Downgrades

## Upgrading
Upgrades take effect immediately after you confirm in Settings > Billing. We charge the prorated difference for the remaining days in the current billing period, and new features unlock right away. Annual customers receive the prorated charge as a line item on the next invoice.

## Downgrading
Downgrades take effect at the end of the current billing period, so you keep your current features until then. If your seat count exceeds the target plan's limit, remove seats before the downgrade date to avoid disruption.

## Proration and credits
When you upgrade mid-cycle, we calculate a prorated charge for the remainder of the period. Unused time on the old plan is applied as a credit against that charge. If the credit is larger than the charge, the remainder is applied to your next invoice.
