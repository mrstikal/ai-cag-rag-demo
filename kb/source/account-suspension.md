# Account Suspension

## Reasons for suspension
DemoDesk suspends an account when it violates the acceptable use policy, sends abusive bulk email, or carries an unpaid invoice past its grace period. Suspensions are a deliberate policy action taken by the trust and safety or billing team; they are not triggered by failed sign-in attempts.

## Difference from a locked account
A locked account is a temporary, automatic protection that clears on its own or with an administrator unlock. A suspended account remains disabled until the underlying issue is resolved. When a suspended user tries to sign in, DemoDesk explains that the workspace or account is suspended and shows the suspension reference number.

## Appealing a suspension
To appeal, reply to the suspension email or open a support case and include your account ID and the reference number from the sign-in message. Trust and safety reviews appeals within three business days. If the suspension is due to an unpaid invoice, paying the outstanding balance reinstates the account automatically within one hour.
