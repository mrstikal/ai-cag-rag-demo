# GDPR Compliance

## Overview

DemoDesk is committed to complying with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) for all customers and users in the European Economic Area. This page explains how we handle personal data, which rights individuals have, and how to exercise them.

For customer workspaces, the customer is the data controller and DemoDesk acts as a data processor. We process personal data only on documented instructions from the controller, except where the law requires otherwise.

## Legal bases for processing

We process personal data based on one of the following legal bases: performance of a contract, legitimate interests, legal obligation, or consent. Account and billing data are processed to provide the service. Security monitoring is based on our legitimate interest in keeping the platform safe. Marketing emails are sent only with consent, which you can withdraw at any time.

## Data subject rights

Individuals have the right to access, rectify, erase, restrict, and object to processing, as well as the right to data portability. You can exercise these rights directly in your account settings or by contacting our privacy team. We verify identity before acting on a request and answer within 30 days.

## Right of access

You can download a copy of your personal data from Settings > Data & Privacy at any time. A formal access request is answered within 30 days. The response includes the categories of data processed, the purposes of processing, and the recipients with whom the data has been shared.

## Right to erasure

You can request deletion of your account, and we remove your personal data once the deletion grace period ends. Workspace owners can delete an entire workspace, which removes all content with it. Some records, such as invoices, are retained where the law requires it. See Account Deletion for details on the grace period.

## Right to rectification

You can correct most personal data yourself in your profile settings. If a record cannot be edited, contact support and we will update it after verification. We notify sub-processors of corrections when the data has already been shared with them.

## Right to data portability

You can export your data in a structured, commonly used, machine-readable format from Settings > Data & Privacy. Exports are provided as JSON and CSV files. See Data Export for delivery times and the scope of the archive.

## Sub-processors

We use a limited set of sub-processors for hosting, email delivery, payment processing, and support tooling. A current list is available in the trust section of the admin settings. We notify customers before adding a new sub-processor and give them the opportunity to object.

## International data transfers

Customer data is stored in the region selected for the workspace. When data is transferred outside the EEA, we rely on Standard Contractual Clauses and additional safeguards such as encryption in transit and at rest. You can request our transfer documentation from the privacy team.

## Data breach notification

If a personal data breach occurs, we notify the relevant supervisory authority within 72 hours of becoming aware of it. Affected customers are notified without undue delay when the breach poses a risk to their rights. Our security team investigates every incident and documents the response.

## Contacting the DPO

Our Data Protection Officer can be reached through the contact form in the app or from your admin settings. We respond to privacy questions within five business days and to formal rights requests within 30 days.
