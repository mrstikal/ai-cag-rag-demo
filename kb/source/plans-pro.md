# Pro Plan

## What's included
Pro costs $49 per agent per month and adds workflow automation, SLA policies, a custom domain for your help center, advanced reporting, and REST API access. Pro also includes priority email support with a 4-hour first response target during business hours.

## Usage limits
Pro supports up to 50 agents and 25,000 new tickets per month, with 50 MB attachments and 100 GB of storage. Ticket history is retained for 24 months, and automations can run up to 10,000 times per month per workspace.

## Upgrading to Pro
Upgrade from Settings > Billing at any time. Upgrades take effect immediately, and we charge a prorated amount for the remainder of the current billing period. Downgrades back to Basic take effect at the end of the current period, so no time is lost or wasted.
