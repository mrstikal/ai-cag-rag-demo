# Payment Methods

## Accepted payment methods
DemoDesk accepts Visa, Mastercard, American Express, and Discover credit and debit cards. Customers in the EU can also pay by SEPA direct debit, and annual Enterprise contracts can be paid by bank transfer. Prepaid cards are not supported for recurring charges.

## Updating a card
Go to Settings > Billing > Payment methods, click Update card, and enter the new card details. The new card is charged on the next billing date. You can keep a backup card on file, and it will be used automatically if the primary card fails.

## Failed payments
If a charge fails, we retry it up to three times over seven days and email the billing contact each time. Access continues during the retry window. If all retries fail, the workspace is suspended until a valid payment method is added and the balance is paid.
