# Workspace Members

## Adding members

Admins add members from Settings > Members by entering an email address and selecting a role. Pending invitations can be resent or revoked at any time. If SSO is enabled, users with a verified company domain join automatically on first sign-in instead of needing an invitation.

## Removing members

Removing a member revokes their access immediately and frees a seat. Their content stays in the workspace and can be reassigned before removal. If you only need to pause access, deactivate the member instead so their history and assignments are preserved. Removed members can be re-invited later.

## Member limits by plan

Free workspaces support up to 5 members. Team plans include 50 seats by default, and Business plans 500. Enterprise agreements define a custom seat count. Guest accounts do not count toward the member limit. When you reach the limit, either upgrade the plan or remove inactive members. Billing is based on active seats, not invitations.
