# Transferring Workspace Ownership

## Transferring ownership

Only the current owner can start a transfer, from Settings > Workspace > Transfer Ownership. Enter the email of the new owner and confirm with your password. The recipient receives an email and must accept within seven days. Until they accept, nothing changes and you remain the owner.

## Requirements

The new owner must be an active member or admin of the workspace with a verified email address. The workspace must have no failed payments or overdue invoices. If SSO is enforced, the new owner must sign in through SSO. Transfers are not available on Free plans; upgrade or contact support for assistance.

## What changes after transfer

Once accepted, the new owner gains billing responsibility and owner-only actions, including deletion and region migration. The previous owner becomes an admin by default, or another role chosen during the transfer. The change is recorded in the audit log, and the workspace plan and members are unchanged.
