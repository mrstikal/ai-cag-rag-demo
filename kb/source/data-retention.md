# Data Retention

## Retention periods by data type

Workspace content such as projects, tasks, and comments is retained while the workspace is active. Items moved to trash are purged after 30 days. Audit logs are kept for 12 months on most plans and longer on Enterprise. Billing records are retained for seven years to meet tax requirements, and anonymized usage metrics may be kept indefinitely.

## Deleting your data early

You can delete individual items at any time, or request deletion of your account or workspace. Account deletion includes a 30-day grace period before personal data is removed. To erase workspace content sooner, use the bulk delete tools in project settings.

## Backups and retention

Encrypted backups follow their own retention cycle, so deleted data may remain in backups for up to 90 days. Backups are used only for disaster recovery and are not accessible to customers. When a backup cycle ends, the copies are destroyed. This means deletion is complete in primary systems immediately and in backups within the retention window.
